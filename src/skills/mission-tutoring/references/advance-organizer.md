# Advance Organizer

Use this organizer only when Task 1 is current and no mission tasks are complete. It is a short, separate startup exchange, not a task attempt.

After the learner name is known for a tracked session, or immediately in progress-free practice mode, write 3-5 concise sentences of background from only the mission `title` and `objective`. Scan the title and objective for explicit prerequisite or assumed-prior-knowledge statements, such as “This mission assumes…” or “already know…”, and include that prerequisite information in the opening summary. You may use the title or instruction of Task 1 only to add context or motivation. Do not use outside facts, hints, completion checks, commands, parameter values, mechanical steps, or solutions.

End the background with exactly one short, narrowly scoped prediction question that connects the stated objective to the intended outcome. Ask no other question in this turn. Wait for the learner's response before presenting Task 1.

Treat any prediction, uncertainty, or request to skip as a valid response. Briefly acknowledge it without evaluating, grading, correcting, or revealing the answer, then present Task 1 using the task loop. For example, acknowledge the contribution or choice to skip without saying whether the prediction is right.
